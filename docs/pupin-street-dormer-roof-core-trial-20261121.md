# Pupin Hall: two street-side dormer roof cores

21 November 2026. Survey-only cumulative supplement to the prior street eave trial. The two provisional dormer cores are independently modeled from NOAA EPT 9306 and compared with NYC 2024 ortho, in the same EPSG:6347 / NAVD88 frame. Preserved rectangles are 1.80 × 1.35 m (west) and 1.80 × 1.51 m (east), not as-built footprints. At V=9.50 m the west/east ridge is ~82.32/82.21 m NAVD88. Swapping source groups 1063–1065 and 1133–1135 yields worst P95 vertical residual 0.178 m over the fit bands.

- Four triangles, a single indexed ridge and one open boundary for each component. Retained and withheld-region probes: 3,510 and 1,472. Blender 4.3.2 reload from final ZIP: all three FBXs and .blend verified; original four components for each LOD compare exact imported signatures for positions, indices, reference UVs, material names and corner normals.
- Roof ends, flashing, walls, connection to host roof, paint/bake, and CS2 Editor not verified. No changes to canonical assets or LFS.
