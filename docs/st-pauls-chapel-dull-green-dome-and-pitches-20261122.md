# St. Paul's Chapel | green tile and east copper, offline LOD2 preview

Open StPaulsChapel-LOD2-Tile-And-Copper-OFFLINE.blend to see the updated packed materials. previous.blend is the prior copper-only version for comparison, not a second delivery choice. This does not replace the existing CS2 LOD2 FBX or production textures. Separate packed texture pages deliberately avoid cross-material mip bleed in the Blender preview; the three-slot material scheme is **not** qualified for the CS2 asset template.

- [Walter B. Melvin Architects' restoration record](https://wbmelvin.com/portfolio-item/st-pauls-chapel/) identifies the dull green glazed Ludowici terra-cotta replacement tiles (T12). [Columbia Facilities' updates](https://cufo.columbia.edu/news/chapelrestoration) distinguish the pitched/dome tiles, copper east apse and flat entrance portico roof. A [2024 city tile](https://tiles.arcgis.com/tiles/yG5s3afENB5iO9fj/arcgis/rest/services/NYC_Orthos_2024/MapServer/tile/19/196939/154430) provides visual texture-zone context only. It is not calibrated BaseColor, a survey of roof joints, or a source of the proposed shader values.
- The previous bake sampled reddish brown (selected texel median about RGB 51/21/13) on the upper dome and three separate pitched-roof assemblies. Corrected 118 edited surfaces on both slopes and the simplified high crown support of the main dome, plus 12 surfaces on the two nave pitches and transept. Preview median about RGB 108/112/79. No change to the 92 copper roof faces or to the other 338 triangles, including lantern, west portico, masonry or unknown setback surface. The model still preserves its rough geometry; no assertion of the physical crown profile, tile spacing, slope or portico material.
- 43,578 interior centers on the selected base UV islands, but new paint resides on a separate texture page entirely. Positions, triangle order, UVs and corner normals all match exactly. The 338 baseline and 92 copper triangles sample the same previous packed BaseColor; all three slots still sample the same verified original normal image. Source and copper shader link signatures unchanged. This does not certify original emissive, mask, roughness, reflectance, mip chain or engine shaders.
- In seven Blender ray views 86,247 probes found 4,933 new tile-slot hits (804 unchanged copper). In the raw UV/top-view diagnostic 105,096 pixels change only on selected roof faces; 13,714 projected copper pixels remain identical. This is offline verification, not a game screenshot or an Editor pass.
- Reproduce with Blender 4.3 and Python NumPy/SciPy/Pillow: paint_green.py recomputes the isolated preview map, then run Blender in background with two threads and --disable-autoexec, --python build_green.py -- FOLDER, then verify_green.py. probe_atlas.py and roof_card.py generate the comparison. No in-game render, collision, asset overwrite, near/mid retexturing, PDX/LFS or production fix.
