# St. Paul's Chapel | East apse copper appearance, offline
The repaired LOD2 shell (308 vertices / 560 triangles) already delivered is unchanged. This trial revises the east apse roof appearance only, as a Blender material-isolation preview. Open StPaulsChapel-LOD2-East-Apse-OFFLINE.blend with packed textures. source.fbx is the unchanged input and is not a texture-patch import. Do not substitute the preview texture for the original CS2 BaseColor, or assume two material slots are supported by this asset's game template.

- Columbia [reports the apse copper roof was installed in 2019](https://cufo.columbia.edu/news/chapelrestoration). Its [restoration architect](https://wbmelvin.com/portfolio-item/st-pauls-chapel/) distinguishes the mostly green-glazed Ludowici terra-cotta roofs. The [2024 city tile](https://tiles.arcgis.com/tiles/yG5s3afENB5iO9fj/arcgis/rest/services/NYC_Orthos_2024/MapServer/tile/19/196939/154430) shows the dark rounded roof on the Amsterdam-facing east. Its exposure varies; this study does not derive true material albedo, copper oxidation, metalness, roughness, seam spacing or roof profile from the photo.
- The actual prior 1024² BaseColor samples on the selected region were reddish brown/cream-flecked (native median [65,34,22]). Selection is 92 upward-facing triangles on the two existing east roof shells (local +Y). It excludes walls, dome, and adjacent pitched/entrance roofs. Preview neutral weathered copper is procedural; the diagnostic median on target native texels is approximately [86,90,94]. It removes the conspicuous cream flecks on those triangles only. Adjacent roof colors in the prior bake are unresolved, not endorsed or redone. No changes to roof shape, eaves or near/mid models.
- The first experiment repainted one atlas at 11,371 native covered texels and up to four pixels of padding; no overlapped UV area. A separating-axis scan of every unaffected face found localized mip-3 and mip-4 color influence with conventional box reconstruction and bilinear sampling, despite isolation at mip levels 0–2. The original mip test is documented in paint-proof.json; the unsafe merged image is deliberately not included. A production solution must maintain mip isolation at actual transition distances.
- In the delivered preview, each of 92 faces instead samples a separate packed albedo on the same UVs. The other 468 faces keep the exact original BaseColor texture; both slots share the exact unchanged original Normal file in Non-Color mode. Geometry, indices, UVs, and corner normals are identical after reopening. The scene is a simplified Blender preview shader, not a certification of original control, emissive and other game inputs. Seven BVH views and 86,247 probes checked exclusivity (804 hits on the new slot); comparison chart is raw albedo projection, not game lighting. The held +13.7 m topology patch remains excluded.
- Tools: uv_world.py dumps the source with Blender; shells.py and mask_probe.py reproduce identification and conservative probe masks; paint.py regenerates only the intentionally rejected single-atlas experiment under a warning filename. apse-isolated.png here is a separate preview map, not an asset channel whose name is accepted by the editor. build_preview.py recreates the preview and verify_preview.py reopens and tests it. Requires Blender 4.3.2 and NumPy/SciPy/Pillow/GDAL for evidence generation. No Editor, game import, Mod publishing, original-file or production repo updates.
