# Pupin Hall LOD1 visual A/B: retain originals

Follow-up to [the 10.1% reduction experiment](pupin-lod1-budget-probe-20261123.md). Matched orthographic front and oblique Blender CPU views (same original BaseColor atlas, camera, simple directional-light proxy, unchanged neutral Win/Gls materials) showed no silhouette difference, but the thinner trim is perceptible as a repeated missing vertical strip in the window pattern. At 300px screen proxies, 1.62% (front) and 2.53% (oblique) of asset pixels differ by >20 in at least one 8-bit RGB channel. The loss is visible even where gross overlap is unchanged. Therefore do not promote the coarse no-strip variant: keep 54,880-triangle medium tier and the current whole dual-dome ZIP unchanged. If actual Editor/game frame-time profiling shows material LOD1 cost, use a separate option with window detail baked into color and normal and compare it in game. Offline render is not a game shader, frame-rate measurement, or validation.
