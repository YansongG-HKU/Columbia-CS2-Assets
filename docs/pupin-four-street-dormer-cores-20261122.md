# Pupin Hall: two outer street-side dormer roof cores
Adds two distinct evidence-backed gable-roof cores to the prior two-dormer package. The editable Blender has 24 mesh objects across three LODs; each FBX now contains eight component meshes. The original six exported components per LOD are reproduced unchanged. These are open patches, not the full Pupin asset, not window walls or flashing, and not an in-game mod.

- The two existing inner cores at roughly local U±12.3 m are kept. The two outer ridge positions at V=9.50 m are U−20.846 and +20.873 m and elevations 82.223 and 82.318 m NAVD88. The outer cores are 1.98 × 1.31 m west and 1.98 × 1.33 m east, 5.2272 m² in horizontal projection combined. This is a supported region, not the outline of an entire dormer. Fits use the existing site frame E587597.892 / N4518181.756 EPSG:6347, +U campus east, +V toward street and local Z0=35.705 m NAVD88.
- [2017 NOAA EPT 9306 LiDAR](https://noaa-nos-coastal-lidar-pds.s3.amazonaws.com/entwine/geoid18/9306/ept.json) first class-1 returns underlie the fits. [NYC 2024 ortho](https://github.com/CityOfNewYork/nyc-geo-metadata/blob/main/Metadata/Metadata_AerialImagery.md) locates the dormers, not their height, flashing or calibrated materials. Fit the two visible slopes on each dormer independently and swap source groups 1063–1065 and 1133–1135 for holdout. Fitted pitches approximately 27–35 degrees, worst conditional P95 vertical residual 0.187 m. Each group covers 4,015 candidate positions per core; largest grid-to-return gap 0.498 m across both cores and groups. These are geometry support metrics, not pointwise field accuracy. Do not infer post-restoration as-built shape from the old survey.
- Each outer core adds 4 triangles with a shared indexed ridge. Fresh FBX and Blender readback verifies original six objects per LOD have identical triangle position, vertex indices, UVs, material name, smooth flag and imported corner-normal fingerprints. All three LODs agree at 3,510 new retained positions; 1,472 deliberately unmodeled probes stay absent. No intersections with the mesh of the modeled wing under this comparison; 781 paired vertical probes give modeled height differences of at least 0.311 m west / 0.386 m east. This is not physical shell clearance. Fit to exported model checks below 0.008 mm measure numerical agreement only.
- To regenerate, run fit_outer.py and combine.py with NumPy/SciPy; then background Blender 4.3 with two threads and --disable-autoexec with build.py or verify.py and this folder as the argument after --. Previous FBXs in previous/ are for readback, not concurrent placement. The ZIP also includes the minimal prior source inputs and plot.
- No wall or flashing additions, collision or real materials, no game/Editor test, no overwriting the approved source, no LFS/main/PDX. Projection or support gaps are intentionally left open.
