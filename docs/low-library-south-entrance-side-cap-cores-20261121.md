# Low Memorial Library | South entrance, two side-cap top cores

**Detached surface components, not a replacement building or a complete wall.** This batch adds two closed, separately named cutout pieces for the measured upper surfaces flanking the entrance stairs. They are positioned in an architectural survey frame. Their 120 mm extrusion exists only to close the files; it is not a wall profile or measured thickness. The neutral preview material and metric UVs are not the original bake. The existing building, its three LODs and all baked maps are unchanged. Planar cap patches need only two top triangles apiece and do not need three geometrically duplicated tiers at this stage.
