# Pupin Hall — two campus-side outer dormer roof cores

This draft receipt does not replace production assets. Two selected portions of two outer campus-side dormers, |U| 20.20–21.74 m and V −6.44 to −5.46 m, 3.0184 m² projected total. Ridge elevation at V −6 m is approximately 82.13 and 82.29 m NAVD88. Local axis and 35.705 m origin copied from prior roof study. This is part of existing Columbia survey reconstruction, with four prior street-side dormer roof cores retained.

From [2017 NOAA EPT 9306](https://noaa-nos-coastal-lidar-pds.s3.amazonaws.com/entwine/geoid18/9306/ept.json), first return class 1, split around source ID 1100. Group-swapped held sample counts are 8–36 per slope and max absolute-z P95 is 0.192 m. Separate group checks over 3,445 selected core locations give max distance to eligible near-surface observations at most 0.440 m. No blind accuracy claim or present-day wall/roof as-built certification.

Every previous object across the three FBX levels (24 total) retains its exact world-space geometry, face indices, UV, material, smoothing and corner normals on reimport. New two roof patches per LOD use two facets with an indexed shared ridge, 8 tris per LOD. 4,386 shared vertical probe locations hit all three LODs with zero observed height delta, 2,080 exterior probes missed the new patches in all three, and no new triangles intersect the earlier geometry. There is no complete roof return or host connection, flashed end, vertical wall or window, and the two inner campus-side positions are not filled. Reopened .blend has 30 mesh objects.
