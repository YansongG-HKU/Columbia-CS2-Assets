# Lerner Hall north glazing and ramps: off-building assembly study

**Not a final reconstruction.** This independently exportable geometry is positioned in its own local axes, not registered to the site's CityGML envelope or any game mesh. It is suitable for evaluating the glazing/translucent ramps and silhouette. It does not prove north facade placement, landing coordinates, entrance grades, material baking, LODs or CS2 Editor import. It does not overwrite the old game mesh, its empty Gls split files, or the central-roof study.

## Controls

- [Arup Journal 2/2000, printed pp. 24–26](https://www.arup.com/globalassets/downloads/arup-journal/the-arup-journal-2000-issue-2.pdf#page=24): three north-side runs rise from Broadway/west toward campus/east; opposite-side concrete ramps run in the opposite direction and are out of scope. Two principal trusses span 100 ft = 30.48 m, between west bearing axis 4 and east axis 7. The drawing Fig. 6 has 17 visually counted glazing bays, not a certified current bill of materials. The glass plane sits approximately 3 ft = 0.9144 m north of the main facade support; the final design thickness is 12+10 mm, with each ramp top made of two 8 mm glass layers. Vertical nominal grid shown here is 8+4+8+4+8+4+10 ft; reverse upper/south glass grid and skylight are out of scope.
- [Hugh Dutton, Perspecta 31, printed pp. 63–65](https://www.usmodernist.org/Y/YP-31.pdf) confirms independent point attachments, no conventional mullions, translucent glass walking surfaces, and two flat mid-slope landings interrupting otherwise inclined ramp runs. The wall seam baseline remains straight-sloped. Connection drawings are used for general appearance, not individual fastener coordinates.
- The elevation sketch compared x around 534 and 1024 in the source page raster: end differences around 26–29 px vertically for the three ramp lines, over 490 px nominal span. This led to a provisional rise **1.68 m** in this relative study only, not the 1.83 m full design site fall and not the 2.47 m difference sampled between exterior ground areas. Three representative deck starting heights (3.14/6.79/10.44 m), width 2.60 m, glass seam gap 6 mm, landing stations, placement of support trusses and guardrail detailing are schematic. Do not treat page-derived axes or GLB origin as surveyed values.
- [Columbia's visitor floor plans](https://lernerhall.columbia.edu/map) use campus left and Broadway right, and are not scaled evidence. Avoid treating room wall, roof corner, or visible lounge boundaries as structural bearing axes. Register later against two visible north wing connections at a shared level, plus a noncollinear depth constraint and an independent held landmark. The two campus and Broadway approaches and north facade are different features; Columbia's completed 2020 storefront work only applies to Broadway entrances.

## Geometry, test, and limitations

The model is split into 119 separate panes, three connected walk surfaces with two flat portions each, and six steel/support detail meshes. No mullion meshes. The clear glass and translucent walk materials contain actual glTF transmission parameters; no texture cards passed off as mesh. Combined GLB has 16,464 triangles, read after re-welding duplicate export vertices to 1 µm precision; every triangle edge is oppositely paired. Independent binary readback measures the two constant-grade sub-intervals of each ramp and checks declared transmission. Structural rods intersect intentionally at connection regions; this is not an engineering model or an assembled building intersection certificate. Color and glass appearance are study materials, not final baked CS2 maps. File uses local X west→east, Blender +Y north, Z as provisional arbitrary local height; GLB uses normal Y-up export.

Regenerate with Blender 4.3.2 as follows (2 threads, no background GPU expectation):
  blender -b -t 2 --disable-autoexec --python-exit-code 1 --python hub_study.py -- OUTPUT_DIR
  python verify_hub_glb.py OUTPUT_DIR/Lerner-north-hub-study.glb

The south circulation, masonry connections, current upper glazing details, exact landings and support arrangement must be independently placed. Then crop/merge scene geometry sensibly; build proof of CS2 glass shader, appropriately simplified corresponding LODs, and Editor validation. Do not claim an Editor import based on this GLB.
