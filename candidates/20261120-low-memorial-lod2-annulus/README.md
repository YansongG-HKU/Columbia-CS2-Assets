# Low Memorial Library LOD2 dome-base gap (offline candidate)

Canonical source: Columbia draft 301fbed54d86dccf8ed6f737e03bd738b1cf3093. The far mesh had exactly eight open indexed edges at local height 32.9 m on the octagonal drum and a separate 24-vertex down-facing cap under the dome. This repair connects the original 8 and 24 perimeters with 32 outward triangles (149.498 m²) and removes 22 now-interior downward triangles. The polygon containment, disjoint triangle coverage, and third-party intersection tests use the entire source FBX. All 428 vertex positions, outer bounds, and retained polygon attributes were preserved. Independent import produced 736 triangles and 1,104 consistently oriented two-face edges, with zero open edges.

Material derives from the corresponding flat upward surface (six triangles) in both source meshes LOD0 and LOD1; their UVs agree, and were fitted in original XY to an affine transform at residual under 3e-8. Four LOD2 maps are patched only inside a previously unused 96x104 RGBA tile at pixel x824,y604, leaving >=16 px guard around repaired UVs. The unchanged emissive map is copied byte-for-byte. All four decoded images outside this tile are unchanged; the normal tangent orientation is matched. Where the far outer strip extends beyond the trusted donor interior (15.45 m apothem), only the nearest homologous edge material is carried outward; this is provisional, not a measured stone treatment. The supplied geometry still preserves the original far outer extents.

Blender 4.3.2, identical front/rear/raised views and both BaseColor and PBR-normal test lighting; all views retained silhouette, and unlit pixels outside the projected repair were identical. In lit renders differences outside that projection are reported separately; possible spill from changed occlusion/shading is not game-tested. Independent decoded-image SHA and exact FBX SHA verification are in manifest.json. Replay with Python, Pillow and zstandard: python replay.py /path/to/real-LFS-checkout /path/to/new-output. PNG output byte hashes may depend on Pillow version; the replay verifies the exact intended RGBA payload. The mesh SHA is always exact.
