# Low Memorial Library and Low Plaza: distinguish the three stair restorations

The canonical 13×10-cell prefab is 104×80 m. Its source scope includes a terrace, stairs and lawns but calls the terrace layout estimated. This candidate note adds **current appearance references**; it does not alter source models, textures or LODs.

- [2022 top-flight work](https://designconstruct.cufo.columbia.edu/content/low-steps-restoration-between-alma-mater-and-low-library): granite ascent from behind Alma Mater up to the Low Library portico; separate behind-statue brick walkway and waterproofing work. Photo may be used for the visible upper staircase, not to infer riser dimensions.
- [2023 lower/middle Low Steps work](https://designconstruct.cufo.columbia.edu/content/low-plaza-granite-steps-restoration): central Low Plaza to Alma Mater ascent and side stairs toward Dodge/Kent. The granite, rails and landing details are not part of the distinct 2026 work.
- [September 2026 completion, photographs and video](https://operations.cufo.columbia.edu/news/columbia-university-completes-full-renovation-low-plaza): College Walk to Low Plaza granite stair flight was replaced using Stoney Creek granite. The adjacent broad plaza uses Pine Hall historic red brick, Stoney Creek granite and Jura Beige limestone. Restored limestone herringbone edge accents, relocated benches, retained fountains, walls and Tennessee Pink Marble urns. The current restored design is not established by a 2024 ortho alone. In CUFO photo IMG_6328 the lower flight is visibly separate from Low Library's portico; LowPlazaPaverAfter confirms the contrast in paving fields, not calibrated albedo.
