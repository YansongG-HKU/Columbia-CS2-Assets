# Columbia Lewisohn Hall | LOD1 eight collapsed ornaments
2026-11-21 04:30 UTC cloud batch. Separate LOD1-body patch and editable source copy only. No replacement of production/main, package merge, game install, LFS upload or Editor pass.

## Root cause and repair
The 2026-11-18 portable LOD1 body's FBX 7500 encoded 16,788 triangles but Blender reread 16,780. The missing eight were not randomly dropped: eight small ornaments each had two triangles with exactly the same vertex IDs and opposite winding. Five pairs had different UVs on the two sides, so blindly deleting the opposite face would have discarded intended sides and left a sheet. The full 7500 near model and the original repository's 7400 LOD1 independently contain the same closed ornaments. Their matched vertex positions agree within 0.0000014 m. The old main LOD1 has 20,966 triangles and is **not** the 16,788-triangle portable baseline.
Replaced the 16 collapsed triangles with eight donor solids, each 12 vertices/20 faces, restoring all eight uprights to their pinned near-tier positions and UVs. Kept the other 16,772 triangle positions, winding, all UV corners and their corner normals bit-for-bit on Blender reimport. The new 7500 FBX has 16,932 triangles, and Blender reads all 16,932, without the eight-face drop. Donors are around façade ornament elevations 9.58–10.36 m in asset-local units. Main model bounds and pivot inputs remain unchanged.

## Independent QA and limits
- Eight repaired solids have 240 opposite-paired edges in total; none intersects another or intersects outside the two existing stone pedestals and two capital sockets. Triangle BVH reported 102 lower seat and 112 upper seat pairs (intentional source-supported joints, not zero global intersections). Other pre-existing body contacts, building scale, entrances, glass, roof and distance switches are outside this patch.
- All five near/mid shared PNGs are unchanged 4096×4096 and decoded. The incoming Blender FBX texture nodes that it exposes (BaseColor, Normal and Emissive) resolved inside the clean trial folder. MaskMap and ControlMask were also independently decoded and hashed. Do not infer the Editor's shader mapping from this. `settings.json` preserves the existing five shared near→mid names.
- The additional .blend contains only the repaired LOD1 body. It reopens with the geometry and relative texture files. It does not replace the full original authoring scene and does not contain later LOD2 geometry or portal edits. For this patch, the provided FBX is the portable 7500 output of the exact annotated array repair; do not re-export the .blend and assume Blender will preserve the same FBX version.
