Source: Columbia draft 783b625b8677b11efcb7409ee3ce02fcdd8f389d, all 19 canonical split LOD2 body FBX LFS hashes verified against the pinned originals (optional dark variant excluded). In Avery, Kent, Lewisohn and Philosophy, overlapping opposite-facing rectangular caps at local X=1.5 m were verified from both directions. The triangles were grouped by shared indexed diagonal rather than face order. Each four-corner perimeter is an exact opposing match; the four attached faces on each side point away from the cap plane; all source triangles were screened for other positive coplanar overlap or any intersection of the open cap interior.

Exactly 16 hidden triangles and 16 duplicate-position vertices removed from these four assets. Closed and consistently oriented edge counts after repair: Avery 252; Kent 156; Lewisohn and Philosophy 138 each. No coordinate movement, exterior bounds unchanged, all raw retained corner UVs, normals, tangent and binormal values and smoothing indices retained. The independent Blender import confirmed retained triangle positions, material slots and UVs at six decimal digits, and maximum normal chord difference below 0.0001 after import quantization. Avery and Kent three-view comparisons sampled unlit BaseColor and lit normal-mapped exterior with the original maps manually attached; see pixels.json.
